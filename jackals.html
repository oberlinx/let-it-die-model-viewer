LET IT DIE Jackals

The Jackals are three hunters sent into the Tower by the "Sunflower Ceremony" company to collect corpses and steal gear for the black market. Each of X, Y and Z comes in five tiers. This page shows every tier's stats, gear, drops and how much damage it takes from each kind of attack, with renders of the game's own models.

What the game says about them

The in-game mails that mention the Jackals, as the game stores them. They are the only place the game names floors for them; the game data itself does not say which tier appears where.

Data comes from the game's master_jackal table (one row per tier) and the tables around it. Each tier's level picks its HP and stats from master_jackal_bodylvl_status_value; the D tiers also carry a "HUD level", which looks like the level shown above them in a fight. Drops: a defeated Jackal leaves one thing, picked with the game's weights per 1,000, shown here as percentages; where the game gives one weight for the whole armour set it is split evenly between the three pieces, as the save editor does. Damage taken comes from master_jackal_param_int for the D tiers and from the JACKAL_X_…, JACKAL_Y_… and JACKAL_Z_… constants for the standard tiers (the game stores those with the Jackal's number, 1 to 3, added to the last digit, which is taken off here), plus the JACKAL_… constants that apply to all of them. Attacks not listed take full damage. The attack names are the game's internal ones made readable, so a label may not match an in-game weapon name exactly. The mushroom mix is the share of each kind among the mushrooms a D-tier Jackal throws. Voice cast is from the game's credits.

Voice: every clip the game has for each Jackal, in English and Japanese, copied as stored (Ogg Vorbis): its combat voice (attacks, getting hit, death and idle chatter, 47 to 54 clips) and the lines it speaks while collecting and when beaten, with the game's subtitles. The game text also holds lines for two more of their scenes whose audio is not in the PC game; those are not included.

Renders show each model in the fighters' neutral idle stance (the Jackals use the same skeleton), lit outside the game engine, so colours are close to but not identical to in-game. The R tiers use the standard model and the D tiers the red-and-black one. The visor glass on Y and on X (D) uses a reflective material in the game and is drawn here as dark tinted glass. The 3D view uses the same meshes with textures reduced to 1024 px.
