LET IT DIE Mushrooms & Beasts

Every mushroom and edible beast in the game data: what it does raw and grilled, which floors it appears on, and which decals it unlocks.

Show

Data comes from the game's master_mushroom and master_beast tables with their effect and spawn tables; icons and artwork from its menu graphics. Floors are the ones whose random spawn list includes the entry; some entries are instead placed at fixed spots in a zone, or only come from beasts, events or the shop, and show no floors. Effect durations are the stored values, taken to be seconds. Not every mushroom has a large artwork or a grilled form.
