LET IT DIE Bosses

The four Dons who hold the Tower of Barbs' stamps, the four mini bosses that roam its floors, and the 4 FORCEMEN who wait in arenas of their own. Each Don fights from a Mk-2 version of one mini boss: Max Sharp rides COEN Mk-2, Colonel Jackson JIN-DIE Mk-2, Mr. Crowley GOTO-9 Mk-2 and Taro Gunkanyama U-10 Mk-2. Renders and a 3D view come from the game's own models; stats, floors and drops from its data.

View

Dons

A Don waits at the end of each part of the Tower and on Tengoku's boss floors. Click one for its fight, its mount's stats, quests and voice.

Mini bosses

The "3 Shock Terrors" COEN, JIN-DIE and GOTO-9, and U-10. Click one for its floors, stats at every level, drops, quests and voice, and to see its Mk-2 version.

4 FORCEMEN

White Steel, Red Napalm, Black Thunder and Pale Wind, each in an arena of its own on three floors of its part of the Tower: Normal, Hard and Nightmare. Click one for its arena floors, stats at every level, damage taken by weapon, gear, quests and voice, and to see its Hell look.

Data comes from the game's master_mboss tables: MBOSS1 to MBOSS4 are COEN, JIN-DIE, GOTO-9 and U-10, and STAGE_BOSS1 to STAGE_BOSS4 are the Mk-2 versions the Dons fight on (the kill quests pair each id with its name). HP, stats, EXP, Kill Coins and SPLithium are listed for every level the data defines; which level you meet depends on the floor (master_floor gives a mini-boss level per floor, shown with the floors). The Don fights are set up in each area's boss arena (master_stage_mboss); the data does not give the Don's own level for a Tower fight, only the Mk-2's levels. Tengoku's boss floors (every fifth floor) can hold any of them.

Drops: on each floor where a mini boss is set up, master_floor_drop_gen gives weights for what it leaves: a crafting material, a group of gear, or group 0, which names no group (what that gives is not clear from the data). Percentages are the weight over the total for that floor; Tengoku's are shown as the range over its floors. A gear group lists the pieces that belong to it; the data does not say how one is picked. The floors listed for a mini boss are the ones with a drop table for it, which is how the data says it can appear there.

Renders show each model in the first frame of its own neutral idle animation, framed to its size and lit outside the game engine, so colours are close to but not identical to in-game. The Dons' models are the parts that sit on their mounts (Gunkanyama's includes his battleship hat). The 3D view uses the same meshes with textures reduced to 1024 px. Voice: every clip the game has for each, in English and Japanese, as stored (Ogg Vorbis). A Don's set includes the sounds made while riding its mount; "Arena" lines are the scenes before and after the fight and "Fight" lines are spoken during it, with the game's subtitles. Voice cast is from the game's credits.

4 FORCEMEN: master_stage_fourforcemen puts each in three arenas of its part of the Tower, at levels 1, 2 and 3; the areas holding them use the normal, HARD and EXPART arena scripts and sit on the floors shown, which match the game's Normal, Hard and Nightmare quests. Levels 4 to 8 are also defined in master_fourforcemen_param_int; the data also has a fourth arena per stage at level 0, which may be the Hell or Tengoku version, but which of those levels it uses is not stated. Attack and defence factors are the game's ATKUP and DEFUP values as stored. They have no character model of their own: in the game each is a fighter wearing its armour set, so the renders put the set (or its Hell version) on the Male 1 body; their weapons are on the gear page. Only Red Napalm's voice actor is named in the credits.
