LET IT DIE Gear

Every weapon, armour piece and crafting material in the game data, with its icon and stats. Each upgrade tier of a piece is listed separately.

Kind

Data comes from the game's master_part and master_item tables and the icons from its UI packages. A tier with no icon of its own borrows the one from the tier below, and gold variants use the plain version's icon; a few have none. The two attack or defence numbers are the pair the game stores for each piece. Blueprints are left out because each one maps to a piece already listed.
