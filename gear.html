LET IT DIE Gear

Every weapon, armour piece and crafting material in the game data, with its icon, stats and what it costs to develop and upgrade. Pieces that upgrade into one another are grouped into one line.

Show

Data comes from the game's master_part, master_item and master_part_research tables and the icons from its UI packages. A tier with no icon of its own borrows the one from the tier below, and gold variants use the plain version's icon; five pieces (such as Fists) have none. The two attack or defence numbers are the pair the game stores for each piece. Upgrade costs per level are worked out from the stored base amounts and growth factors using the same rounding rule the game is known to use for durability; they have not been checked against the game level by level. Blueprints are left out because each one maps to a piece already listed.
